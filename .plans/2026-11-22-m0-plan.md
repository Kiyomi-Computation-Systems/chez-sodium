Date: 2026-11-22

The owner started M0 on 2026-11-22. This plan fills in brief §9, which they approved.

Brief: [the project brief](2026-11-22-chez-sodium-project-brief.md), §7 and §9.

Source: chez-sqlite3 at v0.1.1 (`960d9f0`), at `../chez-sqlite3`. Its own M0 ended at
`d85c28c`, with CI green. That commit shows the shape an M0 harness takes. v0.1.1 holds every

## 1. What M0 delivers

From brief §9:
- the eleven records;
- `progress.md`, `global-constraints.md` and `traps.md`;
- the part of brief §7 that runs with no verb;
- CI's `main`, `memory` and `nothreads` jobs, green;
- `check-ci` holding M0's shape.

`main` has no `make build` and no `check-dev`; they come with the load, in M1.

`src/` holds two libraries:
- `(chez-sodium private require-threaded)`;
- `(chez-sodium private counters)`, which imports only `require-threaded`.

**The empty suite.** `make test` refuses to run with no suites (P-F1). M0's suites are the
harness's own: the testing library's, the counters', `require-threaded`'s, and those of
`check-floors`, `check-nothreads` and `check-ci`, plus `test-manifest`. They make a suite
that is not empty, with no verb in it.

## 2. How each file is made

- **Start from v0.1.1's file, not `d85c28c`'s.** The later file carries the fixes.
- **Trim it to M0.** Use `git diff d85c28c 960d9f0 -- <file>` to tell M0's shape from what
  later milestones added.
- **Rename** `chez-sqlite3` to `chez-sodium`, `CHEZ_SQLITE3_*` to `CHEZ_SODIUM_*`, and
  `sqlite` to `sodium`, where the text means the library.
- **Change only what libsodium needs.**
- **Comments follow AGENTS.md:** they carry no milestone, slice, date or research path.
  - A sibling's record is cited by repository: `chez-sqlite3 ADR-0005`.
  - This project's records are cited as `ADR-NNNN`, by the numbers in §4.

## 5. Order of work

1. **The records** and **the container and harness**, side by side. Their files are
   disjoint.
   - The records: `.plans/decisions/`, `global-constraints.md`, `traps.md`.
   - The container and harness: every other file in §2, up to the memory gate.
   - **Measure first,** on the host, what `b532aa7` gives:
     - Chez's version, and that it is threaded;
     - libsodium's version and soname, and `sodium.h`;
     - gcc and Valgrind.
2. **The memory gate.** Measure first: Valgrind on the container's Chez, loading
   libsodium and calling `sodium_init` through a bare FFI, with no suppression.
3. **CI**, then `check-floors`' workflow rules and the gate's group o, which read the
   workflow.
4. **The ledger**, and the brief's and AGENTS.md's pointers.
5. **A read-only adversarial review** on Opus, over this plan and everything M0 built.

Each check is watched to fail before it is finished. Each piece keeps a mutation log in
`.plans/`, in chez-sqlite3's newer form: Guards, Mutation, Result, Clean rerun.

## 6. Done

M0 is done when all of these hold:

- in the container, `make test`, `make check-test-fails`, `make test-memory` and
  `make check-memory-gate` pass;
- on the host, `make check-guix` passes;
- CI's three jobs pass on the PR;
- the closing review's blockers and majors are fixed, or recorded as owed.
